About the Founder

Banson Asamoah Bright is a Ghanaian entrepreneur and self-taught technology enthusiast with an interest in business, digital marketing and entrepreneurship.

He began exploring entrepreneurship at a young age through trading and other small business activities. His interest in technology and digital platforms later led him to explore coding, marketing and online business.

In 2026, Banson began developing KayaMart with the vision of creating a marketplace where buyers and sellers can connect more conveniently in one place.

About KayaMart

KayaMart is a Ghanaian marketplace designed to connect buyers with entrepreneurs, businesses and store owners. The platform provides a virtual marketplace where sellers can showcase products and reach potential customers.
